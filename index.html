About Us

Watches that look the part without the luxury price

Ocean Pieces started with a simple idea: a good watch should not be reserved for people with a big budget. We pick timepieces with the look of high-end design, check each one before it ships, and price them for everyday buyers.

Whether you are heading to your first job, closing a deal or dressing up for a weekend, we want the watch on your wrist to say something about where you are going.

  • Premium look, fair price

    Classic cases, clean dials and quality straps at prices that make sense.

  • Checked before it ships

    Every watch is inspected and tested so it arrives ready to wear.

  • Delivered to your door

    Choose your watch, pay, share your address and we handle the rest.
